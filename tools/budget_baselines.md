# Budget calibration

These are low-cost builds that completed a Studio simulation with the current
vehicle, obstacle, wind, and material rules. They are verified passing baselines,
not a proof that no cheaper build exists.

| Level | Passing build | Cost | Cost × 1.2 | Nearest $100 | New budget (+$300) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 River Crossing | Short wood deck sections | $640 | $768 | $800 | $1,100 |
| 2 Pinewood Ravine | Wood deck raised over island | $754 | $904.80 | $900 | $1,200 |
| 3 Canal Quarter | Wood deck raised above boat | $868 | $1,041.60 | $1,000 | $1,300 |
| 4 Lighthouse Reach | Short wood deck sections, wind test | $960 | $1,152 | $1,200 | $1,500 |
| 5 Harbor | Two wood ramps, $78 speed jump | $520 | $624 | $600 | $900 |

The Level 5 layout uses a launch joint at (-16, 8), a landing joint at (14, 8),
and two sloped wood deck sections. Coordinates are relative to the level origin.
The failed $500 layout placed its landing joint at (16, 8).
